# Physical SPI Flashing (CH341A)

Deploying IPO_Boot_ROM to physical motherboards requires programming the board's SPI NOR Flash ROM chip. This guide details using the popular and inexpensive **CH341A USB programmer** with `flashrom`.

---

## 🧰 Required Equipment

| Equipment | Purpose | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CH341A Programmer** | USB-to-SPI hardware programmer | Ensure jumper is set to SPI mode (pins 1-2) |
| **SOIC-8 Test Clip (Pomona 5250)** | Connects to soldered SPI chips | Pin 1 marked with red wire |
| **1.8V Voltage Adapter** | Level shifter for 1.8V SPI chips | Mandatory if chip is Winbond W25QxxFW or similar |
| **Multimeter** | Verifies 3.3V / 1.8V VCC pin voltage | Never apply 5V to SPI flash chips! |

---

## ⚡ Critical Electrical Safety Warnings

<Callout type="caution" title="Never Flash a Powered Motherboard">
Always disconnect the target PC's ATX power supply and remove the CR2032 CMOS battery before attaching the SOIC-8 clip! Supplying power from both the ATX rail and the programmer simultaneously will destroy the programmer, motherboard chipset, and flash chip.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Verify Programmer VCC Voltage">
Many cheap black CH341A programmers supply 5V logic levels on data lines despite having a 3.3V jumper! Verify with a multimeter between pin 8 (VCC) and pin 4 (GND) before connecting to expensive motherboards.
</Callout>

---

## 📍 Locating Pin 1 on the SPI Chip

On standard 8-pin SOIC-8 chips:
- **Pin 1:** Identified by a small circular dot/depression in the plastic epoxy package.
- **Pin 8 (VCC):** Located directly opposite Pin 1.
- Align the red stripe on the ribbon cable with Pin 1.

```
SOIC-8 Pinout:
        +---v---+
  /CS  1| o     |8  VCC (3.3V or 1.8V)
   DO  2|       |7  /HOLD
  /WP  3|       |6  CLK
  GND  4|       |5  DI
        +-------+
```

### Steps Explained:
1. **Double Dump Verification:** Taking two separate read dumps (`backup1.bin` and `backup2.bin`) and running `cmp` guarantees that all 8 clip pins have solid, low-resistance electrical contact.
2. **Safe Backup:** Keep your verified factory backup safe! If your custom ROM fails to boot, you can reflash the factory BIOS at any time.
